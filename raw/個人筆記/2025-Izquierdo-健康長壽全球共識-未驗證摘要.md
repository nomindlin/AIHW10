# Global Consensus on Optimal Exercise Recommendations for Enhancing Healthy Longevity in Older Adults

## 1. Core Consensus Overview
This global consensus bridges **geroscience** and **clinical exercise physiology**, positioning structured exercise as a "polypill" that directly targets the biological hallmarks of aging and preserves **Intrinsic Capacity** across the five domains defined by the WHO Integrated Care for Older People (ICOPE) framework: locomotion, vitality, cognition, psychological well-being, and sensory function.

## 2. Key Physiological Mechanisms
* **Targeting Biological Hallmarks of Aging:**
  * *Mitochondrial Function & Biogenesis:* Endurance and high-intensity interval training (HIIT) upregulate PGC-1$\alpha$, enhancing mitochondrial density, mitophagy, and oxidative phosphorylation efficiency.
  * *Cellular Senescence & Genomic Stability:* Regular physical activity reduces senescent cell burden (p16INK4a expression) and attenuates the Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype (SASP).
  * *Autophagy & Proteostasis:* Exercise-induced AMPK activation clears misfolded proteins and damaged organelles across skeletal muscle and neural tissues.
* **Exerkine Network Signaling:** Contracting skeletal muscle secretes endocrine signaling molecules (**exerkines** such as irisin, cathepsin B, BDNF, and IL-6) that cross-talk with bone, adipose tissue, liver, and brain.

## 3. FITT-VP Exercise Prescription
* **Frequency:** 3–5 days/week of aerobic activity; 2–3 days/week of resistance/power training; 3+ days/week of neuromotor/balance training.
* **Intensity:**
  * *Aerobic:* Moderate intensity (60–75% HRmax; RPE 12–13) interspersed with vigorous intervals (80–90% HRmax; RPE 15–16) where medically cleared.
  * *Resistance:* Moderate-to-vigorous (65–85% 1RM).
* **Time:** 150–300 minutes/week of moderate aerobic activity (or 75–150 minutes vigorous), plus 30–45 minutes of resistance/neuromotor work per session.
* **Type:** Precision-matched multimodal training tailored to baseline intrinsic capacity deficits (e.g., prioritizing locomotor/power training for mobility decline; aerobic + dual-tasking for cognitive decline).
* **Volume:** Accumulate $\ge 1,000\text{–}1,500\text{ MET-minutes/week}$ for optimal longevity and all-cause mortality reduction.
* **Progression:** Individualized autoregulation based on daily recovery, sleep, and comorbidity status rather than rigid linear loading.

## 4. Clinical Takeaways
* **Exercise as Primary Prevention for Multimorbidity:** No single pharmacotherapy simultaneously improves cardiovascular reserve, bone mineral density, insulin sensitivity, and neuroplasticity to the degree achieved by combined aerobic and resistance training.
* **Function Over Chronological Age:** Exercise dosage must be prescribed according to biological frailty status and functional reserve rather than chronological age.
* **Sedentary Time Interruption:** Breaking up prolonged sitting every 30–60 minutes with 2–3 minutes of light movement independently improves postprandial glycemic control and vascular endothelial function.
