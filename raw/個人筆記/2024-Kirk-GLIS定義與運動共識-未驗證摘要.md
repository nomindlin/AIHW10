# Global Leadership Initiative in Sarcopenia (GLIS) Conceptual Definition and Exercise Consensus

## 1. Core Consensus Overview
The Global Leadership Initiative in Sarcopenia (GLIS) unified regional diagnostic frameworks (EWGSOP2, AWGS, SDOC) into a single global conceptual definition. GLIS establishes **muscle mass**, **muscle strength**, and **muscle-specific strength** (strength normalized to muscle size) as the core diagnostic components of sarcopenia, while categorizing impaired physical performance (e.g., gait speed, SPPB) as a clinical outcome rather than a diagnostic criterion.

## 2. Key Physiological Mechanisms
* **Addressing Dynapenia & Myosteatosis:** Loss of muscle strength occurs 2–5 times faster than loss of muscle mass due to intramuscular adipose infiltration (**myosteatosis**), impaired excitation-contraction coupling, and reduced neural drive.
* **Mechanical Tension & Satellite Cell Activation:** Heavy-to-moderate progressive resistance training (PRT) generates mechanotransduction signals (via focal adhesion kinase and YAP/TAZ pathways) that activate dormant satellite cells to fuse with existing myofibers, increasing cross-sectional area (CSA).
* **Insulin Sensitivity & Amino Acid Sensitization:** Muscle contraction induces GLUT4 translocation independent of insulin and overcomes age-related "anabolic resistance" to dietary leucine/protein intake.

## 3. FITT-VP Exercise Prescription
* **Frequency:** 2–3 sessions/week per muscle group (minimum 48 hours recovery between sessions).
* **Intensity:**
  * *Primary Hypertrophy & Strength Zone:* 70–85% 1RM (8–12 RM).
  * *Low-Load Alternative (for joint pain/osteoarthritis):* 30–50% 1RM performed close to volitional fatigue (RIR 1–2) to ensure full motor unit recruitment.
* **Time:** 40–50 minutes per session; eccentric phase controlled at 2–3 seconds, concentric phase 1–2 seconds.
* **Type:** Compound multi-joint kinetic chain exercises targeting anti-gravity muscles (squat/sit-to-stand variations, hip hinges, step-ups, rows, chest presses, calf raises).
* **Volume:** 2–4 sets per exercise; 10–16 total weekly sets per major lower-body muscle group for optimal hypertrophy.
* **Progression:** Double-progression model—increase repetitions within the target range (e.g., 8 to 12 reps) at a fixed load, then increase external load by 2.5–5% once the upper rep target is achieved with proper form.

## 4. Clinical Takeaways
* **Diagnostic Clarity:** Clinicians should screen using grip strength and the 5-times Sit-to-Stand (5xSTS) test, confirming low muscle mass via DXA or BIA when indicated.
* **Synergy with Protein Nutrition:** Exercise prescription should be paired with adequate daily protein intake ($1.2\text{–}1.5\text{ g/kg/day}$, distributed at $25\text{–}35\text{ g}$ per meal containing $\ge 2.5\text{–}3\text{ g}$ leucine) to maximize muscle protein accretion.
* **High Effort Matters:** Sub-threshold loading (e.g., casually lifting 1 kg dumbbells without approaching fatigue) fails to recruit high-threshold Type II motor units and will not reverse sarcopenia.
